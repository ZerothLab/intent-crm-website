Every action has a reason.
Every decision has an owner.

Intent connects the whole workflow while keeping evidence, proposals, approvals, and receipts distinct.

  1. 01

    Bring in the evidence

    Capture meeting notes, correspondence, and feedback in the customer workspace. Preserve what was actually said.

  2. 02

    Review the next step

    Verify extracted facts against their sources. Ask the assistant for context, then edit or dismiss its proposal.

  3. 03

    Approve the exact action

    Approval binds the version under review. Changes to content, access, or delivery settings invalidate execution authority.

  4. 04

    Close the loop

    Record receipts, replies, tasks, and commercial outcomes. An uncertain delivery stays blocked until it is reconciled.

Does Intent send data to an AI provider by default?

New workspaces use the deterministic Fixture demo model and simulated delivery. External inference requires an administrator to configure, test, and activate DeepSeek or a compatible model endpoint. Real customer delivery also needs a configured gateway.

What can my team manage?

Customer evidence, fact reviews, private assistant conversations, opportunities, forecasts, tasks, contracts, installments, payments, and refunds. Workspace administrators manage members, four built-in roles, custom fields, integrations, and audit history.

What should I validate before going live?

Validate your model provider, delivery gateway, HTTPS route, access controls, backup restoration, and expected workload. SSO, MFA, invitation and password-recovery emails are not included. A successful local test does not establish production readiness for your infrastructure.

Which languages and business settings are supported?

The interface supports English and Simplified Chinese. Customer evidence retains its original language. Currency is currently CNY and business dates use Asia/Shanghai. Financial entries record events; Intent does not initiate bank transfers or perform revenue recognition.
